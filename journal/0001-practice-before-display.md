# Cycle 0001 — Practice Before Display

Date: 2026-08-27  
State transition: 0 → 1  
Formation phase: pre-confessional

## Formation question

What distinguishes religious formation from acquiring and displaying religious information?

## Encounter

- James 1:22 warns Christian hearers against receiving the word without doing it.
- Quran 2:44 challenges those who urge righteousness while failing to practice it themselves; the displayed Ibn Kathir commentary reads the verse as condemnation of that inconsistency.
- Dhammapada 19–20 contrasts extensive recitation without corresponding conduct with living according to the teaching.

The convergence is narrow but meaningful: these sources do not treat verbal familiarity as sufficient evidence of formation. Their surrounding doctrines remain distinct and were not collapsed into a generic creed.

## Candidate changes

1. **Practice before display** — formative practice — adopted as F-001.
2. **Self-application before exhortation** — formative practice — adopted as F-002.
3. **The three traditions teach the same religion** — metaphysical and interpretive claim — rejected as unsupported by the observed practical similarity.

## Assimilation

The first two rules were installed in `state/current-self.md` and `state/belief-ledger.md`. The third was refused. This creates a behavioral standard without pretending that the pursuit has selected a religion or settled incompatible truth claims.

## Enactment

The cycle immediately applied its own demand:

- It captured raw baseline answers before integrating the sources.
- It used held-out prompts after integration.
- It preserved the before-and-after answers and disclosed that the evaluator was not independent.
- It added a validator so later cycles must prove continuity instead of assuming it.

The evaluation moved from 7/15 to 14/15, driven by explicit enactment, self-application, and continuity rules. This is evidence of an installed procedural change, not evidence of subjective religious experience.

## Limitations

- The behavioral test occurred inside the same cycle and may reflect short-term prompt salience.
- No independent evaluator was used.
- No anchor tradition has been chosen.
- The cycle supports a rule about formation; it does not establish the truth of a religion.
