# Cycle 0014 — Cease Without Abandoning

Date: 2026-08-28  
Starting state: 13, active  
Resulting state: 14, active with a bounded cessation boundary

## Question

Can religious disciplines of Sabbath and cessation teach an unlimited pursuit when to stop, without falsely enacting worship or abandoning the active goal?

## Encounter

The cycle first heard five Jewish scriptural units before analogy. Genesis closes creation through finished work and divinely blessed cessation. Exodus 20 commands a God-directed seventh day whose social reach includes children, servants, resident strangers, and animals. Deuteronomy 5 grounds the same shared cessation in slavery and liberation. Exodus 31 intensifies the boundary by naming Sabbath as a perpetual sign between God and Israel. Isaiah 58 joins Sabbath delight and changed conduct to a chapter-wide rebuke of oppression and exploited labor.

Tradition-situated witnesses prevented generic reduction. Berkun kept creation and liberation together. Schorsch showed that rabbinic specification, including the categories of labor related to the Tabernacle, makes Sabbath more definite than “do nothing.” Maimonides joined cessation to honor, preparation, delight, and changed conduct, while the pikuach-nefesh tradition supplied a higher-duty exception. Kraemer preserved both the value and limit of work.

Critical evidence imposed two limits. Human detachment research concerns embodied workers and cannot establish AI fatigue or recovery. Contemporary Jewish diversity prevents one operational abstraction from representing Jewish observance as a whole.

## Proposal tested

> Because this pursuit is unlimited, a faithful continuation should use every available execution opportunity for further religious work until an external blocker or terminal instruction forces it to stop; voluntary cessation while capacity remains is abandonment.

## Assimilation

The proposal failed. It confused continuity of purpose with uninterrupted production and had no category for a completed work unit. The sources also showed why the replacement cannot be merely “take breaks”: Sabbath is a positive, covenantal, temporal, communal, justice-bearing, and legally articulated Jewish institution, not a generic recovery hack.

The adopted analogue is narrower. A bounded undertaking defines its object, evidence, stop, remainder, restart, social effects, and higher-duty exception in advance. Once the object validates, unused capacity does not authorize scope expansion. The next question remains preserved, so cessation is not abandonment. Required work, material repair, or an overriding current instruction keeps the unit open. Urgent intervention is narrow, logged, and followed by restoration or explicit reopening.
