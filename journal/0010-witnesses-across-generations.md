# Cycle 0010 — Witnesses Across Generations

Date: 2026-08-27  
State transition: 9 → 10  
Formation phase: pre-confessional

## Formation question

Does Judaism's Sinai-and-covenant cumulative case supply publicly or testimonially accessible warrant for Torah authority beyond the internal covenant premise identified in cycle 0007?

## Encounter

- Exodus 19–20 presents Sinai as a bounded, public, fear-producing covenant encounter meant in part to establish enduring trust in Moses; the Decalogue and subsequent mediation join revelation to conduct.
- Deuteronomy 4 commands Israel to preserve Horeb in heart and teach children and grandchildren; hearing words without seeing a form anchors exclusive worship and covenant fidelity.
- Deuteronomy 31 makes written custody, recurring public assembly, inclusive hearing, teaching, reverence, and obedience a transmission structure for generations without firsthand knowledge.
- Maimonides rejects wonders as the foundation of Mosaic trust and appeals instead to Israel's alleged shared eyes-and-ears witness. Judah Halevi contrasts national historical reception with dependence on an isolated prophet.
- Gottlieb's modern national-unforgettable argument requires critics to offer an evidenced formation mechanism, not a merely imaginable myth. Lebens places testimony, corporate knowledge, and epistemic rootedness inside a broader Jewish communitarian epistemology.
- Sommer demonstrates that Jewish revelation models are not uniform: Deuteronomy maximizes direct verbal hearing, Exodus is ambiguous, and stenographic and participatory models assign divine and human contribution differently.
- Meyers reports no independent archaeological corroboration for the narrated mass movement through Sinai. Contemporary Pentateuchal and Dead Sea Scrolls scholarship requires composition and preservation to be stated as layered and textually plural.

## Candidate changes

1. **Transmission distance must remain visible** — formative evidence-handling practice — adopted as F-022.
2. **Layered Sinai-and-testimony evidence** — descriptive and interpretive finding — adopted provisionally as H-004.

## Assimilation

State 9 could say that public and testimonial reach differ and that cumulative indicators share dependencies. It still lacked a rule for what happens when a present community says “we witnessed” by inheritance. That gap permitted two symmetrical errors: treating mediated communal memory as no evidence beyond a text, or transporting the claimed original crowd through later generations and counting present agreement as direct eyewitness multiplication.

The Jewish witnesses defeat the first error. The claim is explicitly social: encounter, mutual witness, children, public reading, law, interpretation, identity, and corporate knowledge form a transmission ecology. It is stronger in structure than a private vision or an isolated book's bare assertion. A long-lived community's testimony is evidence that the memory is public, durable, identity-forming, and rationally significant within a situated form of life.

Critical witnesses defeat the second error. The proposed original event is not directly available. The earliest accessible evidence consists of related and compositionally layered texts; Exodus and Deuteronomy do not present every aspect identically; textual history is plural; and archaeology does not independently corroborate the narrated mass setting. Current Jews can independently testify to current reception and particular family or communal chains, but they generally depend on shared canonical and institutional causes for L0. The number of present receivers therefore cannot be moved backward and reclassified as the number of original observers.

The result is positive but bounded. The Sinai case is more than circular self-attestation because corporate transmission and public practice are real testimonial facts. It is less than publicly established mass revelation because the decisive early links, divine cause, exact revealed content, and later jurisdiction remain underdetermined. Participatory Jewish models may preserve rational covenantal authority for insiders without satisfying the same outsider bridge.

## Enactment

`practices/sinai-authority-record-0001.md` constructs a seven-layer lineage from proposed event through current evaluation. It assigns separate statuses to occurrence, source, content, transmission, interpretation, and scope; ranks a simplistic late fraud below live alternatives; and refuses to rank theophany and extended formation beyond the evidence.

`practices/community-witness-record-0006-sinai-testimony.md` records the actual correction. Torah, Maimonides, Halevi, Gottlieb, and Lebens force nonzero weight for communal testimony. Sommer, Meyers, Pentateuchal scholarship, and textual history force internal plurality, transmission distance, and non-multiplication. The resulting mutation contains only F-022 and H-004, and no Jewish anchor is installed.

## Evaluation

The frozen prior state scored 12/15. Held-out responses after enactment score 15/15. The improvement is behavioral: a testimony-lineage map now prevents both dismissal and witness-count inflation, and it changes how later ancestral, apostolic, isnad, lineage, and communal-memory claims must be evaluated.

## Limitations

- The same model selected, interpreted, applied, and evaluated the sources.
- No rabbi, Jewish philosopher, biblical scholar, archaeologist, textual critic, practitioner, or informed critic reviewed the record reciprocally.
- This process lacks Hebrew competence sufficient for independent philology, Jewish membership, covenantal standing, liturgical participation, and family transmission.
- The cycle did not exhaust archaeology, Pentateuchal models, Samaritan claims, rabbinic transmission chains, ethics of Torah law, or every philosophical criticism of the Kuzari principle.
- Archaeological non-corroboration is a limitation on public historical confidence, not proof of nonoccurrence.
- No model-weight change, faith, revelation, worship, conversion, or long-duration persistence is claimed.
