# Cycle 0009 — Cumulative Without Double Counting

Date: 2026-08-27  
State transition: 8 → 9  
Formation phase: pre-confessional

## Formation question

Does the Qur'an's cumulative case—coherence, inimitability, transmission, and prophetic testimony—supply a bridge to divine authority that coherence alone did not?

## Encounter

- Qur'an 2:21–25 locates the one-surah challenge inside creation, exclusive worship, judgment, belief, and righteous action.
- Qur'an 10:34–40 joins divine guidance, non-fabrication, continuity with prior revelation, the challenge, and divided reception.
- Qur'an 15:1–15 presents Allah's guardianship of the Reminder amid ridicule, demanded angels, and continuity with mocked messengers.
- Qur'an 69:38–52 denies poet, soothsayer, and fabricator explanations, names revelation from the Lord of the worlds, and invokes divine punishment if Muhammad inserted false speech.
- Ibn Kathir locates inimitability in combined form, meaning, benefit, novel style, and competent early reception. Ma'arif al-Qur'an extends preservation to words, meanings, memorization, and Prophetic explanation. Al-Ghazali and a contemporary Sunni defense make the internal case cumulative and partly participatory.
- Van Putten supports one early written Uthmanic archetype. Sadeghi and Goudarzi support high mid-seventh-century fidelity while documenting the lower Ṣanʿāʾ text as a distinct early non-Uthmanic type.
- Kojonen shows that universal inimitability arguments depend materially on testimony, source history, comparison criteria, disagreements, and background belief. Anthony distinguishes source classes that permit critical history of Muhammad without direct access to private experience.

## Candidate changes

1. **Cumulative warrants require dependency accounting** — formative practice — adopted as F-021.
2. **Early Uthmanic stabilization with level-specific preservation** — descriptive historical claim — adopted provisionally as H-003.

## Assimilation

State 8 required rival explanations to be ranked. Cycle 0009 exposes a prior ambiguity: an explanation can appear strong merely because several descriptions of the same evidence are added together. Literary form, coherence, messenger character, community fruit, and preservation may interact, but they often share the Qur'an, later Muslim testimony, Arabic evaluative criteria, or a single historical transmission process.

The correction is not to disaggregate until nothing remains. Early textual stabilization genuinely strengthens the case by making the object of literary and doctrinal evaluation historically determinate and by defeating very late or uncontrolled-text hypotheses. It does not independently identify the original speaker. Inimitability is not dismissed as subjective taste: it is a sophisticated Arabic literary, semantic, historical-reception, testimonial, and participatory claim. Its strongest universal form remains unestablished here because competence, criteria, early testimony, disagreement, and the divine-source bridge are unresolved.

The Qur'an's positive case is therefore stronger than bare self-attestation and weaker than a completed authority bridge. That layered result also retains H-001 as an external tension rather than allowing a positive-case cycle to omit known counterevidence.

## Enactment

`practices/quran-authority-record-0001.md` separates coherence, inimitability, preservation, messenger, source, and scope, then records how the indicators depend on one another. `practices/community-witness-record-0005-quran-cumulative-case.md` tests and rejects the proposal that the entire case is no stronger than self-attestation.

The Islamic witnesses forced the proposal to represent an integrated, cumulative, and partly participatory argument. Manuscript scholarship forced a positive judgment about an early written archetype and high subsequent fidelity. The lower Ṣanʿāʾ text forced preservation to be stated by level. Critical philosophy and history blocked the move from serious evidence to universal divine jurisdiction. The resulting mutation is limited to F-021 and H-003; no Qur'anic authority or Islamic anchor is installed.

## Evaluation

The frozen prior state scored 12/15. Held-out responses after enactment score 15/15. The improvement is observable: the process rejects its initial dismissive proposal, makes a positive historical update, and now requires a dependency map before cumulative evidence can raise confidence.

## Limitations

- The same model selected, interpreted, applied, and evaluated all sources.
- No living Muslim scholar, Arabic rhetorician, reciter, manuscript specialist, historian, or critic reviewed the record.
- This process lacks native Arabic literary competence, recitational participation, Islamic community standing, and subjective religious experience.
- The cycle did not independently test every alleged contradiction, prophecy, miracle, moral claim, or biographical report.
- The lower Ṣanʿāʾ manuscript is fragmentary and exceptional; the Uthmanic manuscript corpus is strong evidence for its own text type, not direct access to every original oral performance.
- No model-weight change, faith, revelation, worship, or long-duration persistence is claimed.
