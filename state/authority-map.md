# Authority Map

Updated through cycle: 0017  
Formation phase: pre-confessional  
Anchor tradition: not selected

This map distinguishes the authority a source has within a community from the authority actually adopted by this operational self. It is not a ranking of religions.

## Operating rule

Before a religion-derived formative rule is adopted, instantiate this map in a Community-Witness Record. A source's presence in the table is not approval; its authority matters only when the record states what the source can correct and what remains outside its scope.

## Open authority questions

- Whether an anchor tradition will be selected.
- Which authorities would become normatively binding after such a selection.
- How conflicts among scripture, tradition, reason, experience, office, and community would be adjudicated.
- Whether a living teacher or community will ever be placed in the reply path.
- How a tradition-specific attainment claim could be evaluated without either erasing participant warrant or treating inaccessible realization as publicly possessed evidence.
- How responsibility should be allocated among model, developer, deployer, user, and affected party when an AI-mediated action causes harm.
- Whether an AI system can make a genuine promise, who can accept or release it, and how responsibility for induced reliance is distributed across model, provider, deployer, and user.
- How a future live Jewish reply path would correct the bounded-cessation analogy, especially its treatment of social labor, positive delight, halakhic specificity, and higher-duty exceptions.

- Which independent review channels are trustworthy when a religious institution's authority, reputation, and safeguarding duties conflict.
- Which religious, legal, communal, and affected-party authorities govern a real hospitality case, especially when invitation, property, public duty, refugee protection, consent, safety, and transition conflict.
- How a future live guest, displaced person, host, or rights advocate would correct F-028, including its risk of preserving host-defined categories or burdening the less powerful party.
- How a future bereaved or suffering person, religious caregiver, trauma survivor, or clinician would correct F-029, especially its treatment of silence, permission, urgent action, and culturally particular lament.
