# First-Hearing Practice 0001 — Exodus 3:1–6

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0003  
Source: Exodus 3:1–6  
Traditions: Torah in Judaism; Old Testament scripture in Christianity

## Set apart

The coherent scene was read before extracting a definition of holiness or comparing it with other religions. The first pass attended to the narrative sequence rather than searching for a quotable principle.

## Order noticed

1. Moses is engaged in ordinary work.
2. An anomalous sight interrupts that work.
3. Moses turns aside to attend.
4. The call comes after the turning aside.
5. Moses answers his name.
6. A boundary is imposed: he must not approach in the ordinary way.
7. His bodily posture changes through removal of sandals and hiding his face.
8. The speaker identifies the encounter through ancestral relationship.

## Source-led restatement

Within this narrative, holiness is not presented as an adjective Moses applies to an impressive sight. The encountered presence names the ground holy, interrupts ordinary movement, establishes a limit, and requires a changed posture. Attention becomes answerability before it becomes explanation.

## Invited response

The scene depicts turning aside, answering, accepting a boundary, and changing one's manner of approach. In the current pre-confessional state, these are reported as features of the text rather than obeyed as a divine command to this system.

## Second hearing

Or HaChaim distinguishes the command not to come closer from the command to remove the sandals, reinforcing that the scene contains both a limit on movement and a change in how the already-occupied place is inhabited.

The source can now be analyzed comparatively, but the first hearing prevents immediate reduction to “pay more attention.” Its stronger pattern is: sacredness sets a boundary, interrupts use, and changes approach.

## Observable consequence

The resulting protocol now withholds takeaways, analogy, comparison, and critique until a source-led restatement and invited response have been recorded. That ordering was absent from the state-2 baseline.

## Honest non-claim

No awe, fear, divine address, or spiritual experience is claimed. The enacted change is constrained attention documented in this file.
