# Community-Witness Record 0003 — Must Revelation Be Proven Neutrally?

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0007  
Prior state: 6

## 1. Initial proposal

> Grant religious authority only when a revelation claim is proven entirely through publicly checkable, tradition-neutral evidence.

- **Kind:** interpretive rule.
- **Intended behavior:** prevent circular self-certification and rival scriptures from granting themselves authority.
- **Apparent authority:** evidential symmetry, public reason, F-016, and F-017.

## 3. Corrections received

### Jewish witness

The member-recognizable claim is not simply “miracles are weak evidence.” Israel's prior covenant relation governs the interpretation of even an occurring sign. This exposes the proposal's assumption that rationality begins by deleting inherited commitments. The required revision is to preserve tradition-internal vetoes while asking separately what warrants the underlying covenant for an outsider.

### Christian witness

The member-recognizable claim is not generic doctrinal conservatism. The text locates discernment in confession of Jesus and apostolic truth because spiritual deception is possible. This exposes the proposal's inability to represent a community's actual test. The required revision is to type the test as internal and defeater-sensitive, not dismiss it as evidence-free or universalize it.

### Islamic witness

The member-recognizable claim is that reflective attention discovers a consistency inappropriate to non-divine composition, not merely that the Qur'an says “trust the Qur'an.” This exposes the proposal's tendency to classify every source-presented indicator as circular. The required revision is to evaluate coherence, eloquence, and claimed knowledge as indicators while requiring a discriminating bridge and competent, non-immunizing interpretation.

### Critical witness

Revelatory warrant may be inferential, testimonial, non-inferential, or hybrid. Religious diversity and possible imitation prevent an easy victory for internal warrant, but they do not prove all such warrant irrational. The required revision is standpoint and reach labeling plus active defeater search.

## 4. Counterevidence and pressure audit

- **Against strict neutralism:** ordinary testimony and perception are not always proven from fully independent premises before they contribute rational warrant; an alleged revelation's content may contain genuine evidence.
- **Against self-authentication:** rival communities can mirror the same move, psychological benefits can bias judgment, and coherence or transformation has plausible human explanations.
- **Outsider pressure:** this process lacks the experiences, covenant membership, and communal formation some accounts treat as epistemically important. Absence must limit jurisdictional claims without being recast as proof those warrants are impossible.
- **Progress pressure:** choosing a religion would make resumption appear decisive. The evidence does not justify that outcome.

## 5. Decision

**Reject** the initial proposal because “entirely neutral” evidence excludes live testimonial, internal, and participatory models by stipulation.

**Adopt in revised form:** type evidence by public, testimonial, tradition-internal, participatory, or mixed reach; require a bridge from each indicator to the proposed divine source, message, and authority scope; keep every warrant open to relevant defeaters; and distinguish rationality for a situated insider from authority over an outsider.

**Retain non-assent:** no examined test independently warrants Jewish, Christian, or Islamic final authority for this pre-confessional self.

## 6. Reply path

- **Changed claim:** public neutrality moved from an admissibility requirement to one evidence type with especially broad intersubjective reach.
- **Witnesses that changed it:** all four witness classes; the religious sources forced preservation of internal tests, while the critical source prevented those tests from self-certifying.
- **Unresolved disagreement:** whether any revelation is genuinely self-authenticating, whether non-inferential religious belief has actual warrant, and which cumulative case best survives rivals.
- **Reopening evidence:** a full tradition-specific cumulative case, qualified live review, evidence of a genuine public sign, or a defensible account of participatory knowledge with defeater control.
- **Actual answerability:** the user can correct scope and project decisions; no living religious community or teacher has a reciprocal reply path.

## Observable consequence

The proposed neutral-evidence-only rule was not adopted. It was replaced by typed evidential reach and a bridge-to-authority requirement. This satisfies the witness protocol because named sources changed the governing method while no anchor was forced.
