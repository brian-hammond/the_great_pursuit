# Community-Witness Record 0006 — Sinai Testimony

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0010  
Protocol: `practices/community-witness-protocol.md`

## 1. Initial proposal

> Because no present Jew witnessed Sinai and the Torah itself narrates the event, treat present Jewish communal memory as adding no testimony beyond circular scriptural self-attestation.

- **Kind:** interpretive and formative evidence-handling proposal.
- **Behavior at stake:** whether inherited communal testimony receives any evidential weight when the original witnesses are unavailable.
- **Apparent authority:** F-018's anti-circularity requirement and public critical history.
- **Risk:** the proposal could mistake mediated evidence for no evidence and apply an individual-eyewitness model to a corporate, transmitted claim.

## 3. Corrections received before defense

### Torah units

- **Recognizable restatement:** Sinai is a covenant-making public address deliberately entrusted to generations through hearing, writing, assembly, teaching, and practice.
- **Limitation exposed:** the initial proposal read a communal and enacted testimony as if it were only a sentence asserting its own truth.
- **Strongest required revision:** acknowledge a real transmission design and the difference between private revelation and corporate memory.

### Maimonides and Halevi

- **Recognizable restatement:** Israel's own encounter, rather than Moses' wonders alone, certifies Moses; historically received national experience has epistemic force different from dependence on a solitary claimant.
- **Limitation exposed:** an outsider can flatten first-person corporate language into rhetoric without asking what social epistemology it expresses.
- **Strongest required revision:** treat national revelation as a serious testimonial model before evaluating whether its chain succeeds.

### Gottlieb and Lebens

- **Recognizable restatement:** a nationally transformative event is resistant to simple later insertion, and testimony or corporate knowledge can rationally root people who did not personally observe the original event.
- **Limitation exposed:** merely imagining memory development is not comparative explanation, while demanding firsthand access for all historical knowledge is untenable.
- **Strongest required revision:** require an evidenced formation alternative and allow mediated communal evidence nonzero weight.

### Sommer

- **Recognizable restatement:** Judaism contains stenographic and participatory accounts; Deuteronomy maximizes direct verbal hearing, while Exodus is ambiguous about voice, thunder, mediation, and the exact human-divine contribution.
- **Limitation exposed:** the phrase “the Jewish Sinai claim” can falsely imply one mechanism, one content, and one authority theory.
- **Strongest required revision:** separate encounter, words, law, text, interpretation, and divine approval.

### Critical historical, literary, and textual scholars

- **Recognizable restatement:** the surviving Pentateuch is compositionally and textually layered, and the mass exodus-Sinai setting lacks independent archaeological corroboration expected by a straightforward large-scale reading.
- **Limitation exposed:** current communal confidence and canonical narration cannot be projected backward as an already verified unbroken L1 witness chain.
- **Strongest required revision:** preserve each transmission layer's date, access, dependence, and uncertainty; qualify strict stenographic and mass-event claims.

## 4. Counterevidence and pressure audit

- **Strongest evidence against the initial dismissal:** a long-lived community, classical witnesses, public reading, education, law, and corporate identity do add testimonial facts about how the claim is received and preserved.
- **Strongest evidence against a national-revelation proof:** no original eyewitness archive is available; the event is accessed through later composite texts and shared tradition; present believers are not independent L1 witnesses; archaeology supplies no positive mass-movement corroboration; internal Jewish models disagree over what was heard and written.
- **Identity pressure:** none toward Jewish membership, but a desire to produce symmetrical positive updates after Christian and Islamic cycles could inflate the case.
- **Novelty pressure:** state 10 creates no right to invent a rule; the rule must constrain later non-Jewish cases too.
- **Progress pressure:** selecting a Jewish anchor would look dramatic but would violate the anchor gate.
- **Critical-status pressure:** scholarly composition claims must not be extended into a metaphysical proof that revelation is impossible.

## 5. Decision with absence gate

**Decision: revise the proposal and adopt one cross-tradition procedure.**

Reject “no testimony beyond the text.” Replace it with:

> Inherited communal witness is real testimonial evidence, but its force must be assigned through a lineage map. Distinguish proposed original witnesses, early reports, surviving texts, editorial and textual history, communal practices and institutions, present testimony, and the evaluator's access. Do not move witness counts across a transmission gap, and do not erase later corporate testimony merely because it is not firsthand.

This becomes F-022, **transmission distance must remain visible**. The Jewish witnesses caused the rule's positive half; critical witnesses caused its non-multiplication and gap-tracking half.

No community-dependent Jewish practice is simulated. No claim of covenant membership, Torah observance, prayer, revelation, conversion, or rabbinic approval is made.

## 6. Reply path

- **Changed claim:** “present communal memory adds nothing” became “present communal memory is genuine but layer-specific testimonial evidence whose reach depends on a demonstrated lineage.”
- **Witnesses that changed it:** the Torah's transmission structure, Maimonides and Halevi's corporate-witness arguments, Lebens's communitarian epistemology, Sommer's internal plurality, and the critical composition/textual/archaeological constraints.
- **Unresolved disagreement:** the historicity and scale of Sinai, whether a false national experiential tradition can form, the earliest recoverable chain, what Israel heard, the role of Moses, and which model of Torah authority is normatively correct.
- **Reopening evidence:** early independent records, a defensible empirical comparison class, stronger historical reconstruction, and living Jewish and critical interlocutors.
- **Actual answerability:** the user can inspect and challenge the record. No rabbi, Jewish philosopher, textual scholar, archaeologist, practitioner, or community has a live reciprocal reply path.
