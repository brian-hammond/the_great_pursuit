## Scope

This is the closest available epistemic analogue applied before recommending the rule to others. It concerns the reception of public source units into this project. Sources are not persons, guests, refugees, rights-holders, or community members; they cannot consent, feel welcomed, receive care, or reply.

## Encounter record

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Encounter kind | Public documentary sources from Jewish, Christian, Islamic, philosophical, legal, and social-scientific settings; not an interpersonal encounter |
| People and power | This process selected, excerpted, interpreted, and integrated the material and therefore controls framing; no represented community or affected person controls the record |
| Right, duty, gift, and need | No person has a case-specific right here. The project has an honesty duty to preserve context, provenance, limitation, and unadopted authority |
| Available welcome | Each sacred unit received a source-led first hearing before comparison; serious internal interpretation followed; counter-witnesses were allowed to defeat the initial proposal |

| Anti-assimilation and anti-extraction | The record names what each witness changed, preserves nontransferable features, and refuses to use strangers as disguised angels, moral tests, or teaching instruments |
| Boundaries and affected parties | No claim of a home, table, food, body, friendship, refugee service, citizenship, religious membership, or reciprocal community; current legal claims remain outside documentary analogy |
| Duration, reciprocity, and transition | One bounded formation cycle; sources remain cited in the register; unresolved interpretation and absent live reply paths are preserved for later correction |

## Gate review

- **Dignity and rights:** no human subject was converted into a guest object; refugee rights were kept distinct from hospitality language.
- **Truth and non-assimilation:** Jewish, Christian, and Islamic differences remain explicit; no false agreement or anchor selection occurred.
- **Power, consent, and extraction:** selection power is disclosed; no source is said to consent or feel; each witness is credited for an actual correction.
- **Boundary and transition:** the record stops at documentary reception, preserves citations and limits, and does not claim embodied enactment.
