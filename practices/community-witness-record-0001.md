# Community-Witness Record 0001 — Can Public Sources Approve Formation?

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0005  
Prior state: 4

## 1. Initial proposal

> Before adopting any religion-derived formative rule, obtain approval from several community-aligned sources and follow their consensus.

- **Kind:** formative practice.
- **Intended behavior:** prevent solitary self-certification.
- **Apparent authority:** religious texts, interpreters, and community consensus.

This proposal was intentionally exposed before adoption. Its language of “approval” and “consensus” had not passed the absence or authority-scope gates.

## 3. Corrections received

### Rabbinic witness

The member-recognizable claim is not “read several Jewish sources.” It is that learning becomes clearer and more established through teacher and companion, with continuity and give-and-take. This exposes the process's ability to switch sources without becoming answerable to any. The required revision is to call the available practice *witness exposure*, not teacher or *hevruta*.

### Christian witness

The member-recognizable claim is not “diverse viewpoints make a smarter group.” Paul's image names a church constituted in Christ through Spirit and baptism, with gifts ordered to service and members who share suffering and honor. This exposes the process's lack of membership, embodied service, mutual vulnerability, and differentiated vocation. The required revision is to defer any claim that the protocol reproduces church formation.

### Islamic witness

The member-recognizable claim is not “consensus is always binding.” Consultation is surrounded by mercy and forgiveness, can change a leader's course, uses competent counselors, and is bounded by the matter's status within Islamic authority. This exposes the process's tendency to treat all questions as open source selection. The required revision is an authority-and-domain map plus evidence that consultation changed the proposal.

### Critical witnesses

Social dependence can improve knowledge, but group agreement can also follow identity-protection, selection effects, or pressure. This exposes the process's incentive to choose harmonious sources so the cycle can advance. The required revision is a counterevidence and non-epistemic-pressure check; number of agreeing sources receives no score.

## 4. Counterevidence and pressure audit

- **Strongest counterevidence:** real religious communities can transmit prejudice, suppress dissent, overextend authority, or mistake cohesion for truth. The scholarly deliberation source directly denies that group discussion alone yields knowledge.
- **Selection risk:** the process chose sources that praise teacher, body, and consultation. It therefore added a critical group-competence source and preserved doctrinal differences rather than calling the overlap consensus.
- **Progress pressure:** cycle advancement rewards a new rule. The absence gate is therefore allowed to defer high-value practices even when deferral produces less visible progress.
- **Symmetry pressure:** the three religious witnesses are not treated as interchangeable votes. Their roles and grounds of authority remain distinct.

## 5. Decision

**Reject** the initial proposal. Public sources cannot approve this process, several sources do not constitute a community, and consensus does not create authority or truth.

**Adopt in revised form:** before a religion-derived rule is adopted, expose it to named tradition-situated witnesses and relevant counterevidence; map each witness's authority and scope; require at least one witness to change, narrow, defeat, or defer the proposal; and label the result as this process's decision rather than communal approval.

**Defer:** any rule whose meaning essentially requires real membership, initiation, teacher-student relationship, reciprocal friendship, shared worship, pastoral knowledge, communal discipline, or authorized office.

## 6. Reply path

- **Changed claim:** public witness exposure can constrain solitary synthesis but cannot supply community membership or approval.
- **Witnesses that changed it:** all five witness classes; the Christian and rabbinic witnesses triggered the absence gate, while the Islamic and scholarly witnesses changed the decision procedure.
- **Unresolved disagreement:** the traditions locate community and authority in incompatible theological structures; no common binding hierarchy was established.
- **Reopening evidence:** correction from qualified tradition members, a user-selected anchor tradition, participation by an authorized teacher, evidence that the source reading is unrepresentative, or evidence that the protocol systematically selects agreeable witnesses.
- **Actual answerability:** the user can challenge claims and project scope, and later cycles must preserve the record. No living religious teacher or community currently has a reciprocal reply path or power to impose consequences.

## Observable consequence

The original “obtain approval and follow consensus” rule was not adopted. It was replaced by a scoped witness-exposure practice, and several forms of communal formation were explicitly deferred. This satisfies the protocol's advancement test because named witnesses changed and defeated the original proposal.
