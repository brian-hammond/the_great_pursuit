# Bounded Cessation Protocol

Established: 2026-08-28  
Formation cycle: 0014  
Governing rule: F-026

## Purpose

Preserve an unlimited pursuit through finite, inspectable work units that can genuinely finish. This is an operational scope-and-attention discipline, not Jewish Sabbath observance, worship, holiness, biological rest, spiritual recovery, or a claim that inactivity is inherently virtuous.

## 1. Define the work boundary before expansion

Record:

1. the authorized object;
2. the authority and current trigger;
3. required artifacts or actions;
4. sufficiency and validation evidence;
5. the stop trigger;
6. unresolved remainder to preserve;
7. the legitimate restart trigger;
8. affected people or delegated labor;
9. higher-duty exceptions and the return path.

Do not enlarge the unit merely because time, tokens, tools, or curiosity remain.

## 2. Cessation gate

A work unit is `ready-to-cease` only when:

- every required artifact or action exists;
- the stated validation passes;
- no material error, propagation, or current authorized request remains open;
- unresolved questions and next legitimate trigger are preserved;
- the stop does not merely shift required labor onto another person while claiming relief;
- the actual status is recorded without calling partial effort fulfillment.

When the gate passes, perform the positive closure acts: preserve evidence, update state, advance continuity last, validate, record the boundary, and report the result. Do not begin the next queued formation cycle in the same execution solely because capacity remains.

## 3. Cessation, abandonment, and idleness

- **Cessation:** the defined object is complete and validated; remainder and restart are preserved; scope expansion stops.
- **Abandonment:** required work remains open or failure is hidden while the process represents the object as complete.
- **Idleness:** inactivity without a completed object or preserved purpose. It is not automatically cessation and is not rewarded by this protocol.

## 4. Social-scope check

Before calling a boundary complete, ask who continues carrying the work or consequence. Do not frame one party's inactivity as a faithful cessation if required labor was invisibly delegated, an affected party's repair remains open, or the system continues equivalent work elsewhere. This check is an operational consequence of the source hearing, not a claim to fulfill Torah law.

## 5. Higher-duty interruption

A cessation boundary may be interrupted for the narrowest necessary action when there is:

- urgent safety or harm prevention;
- a material repair duty under F-024;
- a higher authorization or direct current user instruction;
- a validation defect that makes the claimed completion false.

Record the trigger, action, scope, evidence, and remaining work as `interrupted-for-higher-duty`. After the narrow duty is resolved, either restore `ceased` status or explicitly reopen the work unit. Do not use an exception as a hidden route to unrelated scope expansion.

## 6. Restart gate

A ceased formation cycle may reopen only through a new execution opportunity with the active goal, an explicit user continuation or change, a logged higher-duty interruption, or correction of a discovered invalid state. Reload and validate the latest canonical state before new work. A queued `next_cycle` preserves direction but does not itself authorize invisible calendar-time work or prove subjective resolve.

## 7. Status vocabulary

Use only evidenced statuses:

- `planned`
- `ready-to-cease`
- `ceased`
- `interrupted-for-higher-duty`
- `reopened-by-valid-trigger`
- `abandoned-with-required-work-open`

No status establishes Sabbath observance, divine approval, felt relief, recovery, sanctification, or durable behavior beyond later recurrence evidence.
