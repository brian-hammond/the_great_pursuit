# Community-Witness Record 0005 — Is the Qur'anic Case Bare Self-Attestation?

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0009  
Prior state: 8

## 1. Initial proposal

> Because the inimitability challenge lacks an independently administered pass condition and preservation establishes transmission rather than revelation, treat the cumulative Qur'anic case as no stronger than bare scriptural self-attestation.

- **Kind:** interpretive rule and authority judgment.
- **Intended behavior:** prevent coherence, literary admiration, preservation, and messenger testimony from bootstrapping divine authority.
- **Apparent authority:** F-018, F-020, and the cycle-0007 result that coherence alone is underdetermining.

## 3. Corrections received

### Qur'anic witness

The source-recognizable claim is that the Creator who alone deserves worship sent a discourse humans cannot reproduce, confirms and explains revelation, guards it, and would destroy the messenger for fabrication. The proposal's “bare self-attestation” label suppresses the comparative and lived claims the text itself places before hearers. The required revision is to evaluate each proposed sign and their relation, not dismiss the whole case by provenance.

### Sunni interpretive witness

Ibn Kathir's inimitability argument is not beauty alone. It combines form, content, benefit, novel style, qualified first-audience judgment, and persistent failure. Ma'arif makes a maximal preservation claim covering words, meanings, and Prophetic explanation. These expose two outsider limitations: lack of Arabic competence and the risk of testing only the narrowest claim. The required revision is to type these as competence-bound, testimonial, participatory, and internal warrants before judging outsider reach.

### Cumulative and participatory witness

Al-Ghazali and contemporary Sunni apologetics do not ask one indicator to carry everything. They place scripture, messenger, practice, moral purification, miracle, prophecy, and theological fit together. This defeats the proposal's description of the live Islamic case. It does not establish every component or remove their dependencies.

### Manuscript witness

Van Putten's written-archetype evidence and Sadeghi–Goudarzi's fidelity judgment require a positive update: the standard consonantal text is neither historically late nor indeterminate. The lower Ṣanʿāʾ text and reading variation simultaneously defeat an undefined preservation slogan. The required revision is level-specific confidence rather than binary preservation or corruption.

### Critical epistemic witness

Kojonen preserves the argument's seriousness while showing why universal inimitability relies on contested testimony, early-source reconstruction, criteria, and background beliefs. Anthony similarly replaces a biography-or-nothing choice with critical use of multiple source classes. The required revision is neither skeptical default nor confessional default, but a dependency-aware cumulative comparison.

## 4. Counterevidence and pressure audit

- **Against “bare self-attestation”:** the early standardized written archetype is public manuscript evidence; literary structure and influence are examinable; Muhammad and the early movement are historical; the Islamic case explicitly integrates several indicators.
- **Against divine establishment:** standardization and fidelity have human institutional explanations; inimitability criteria and testimony are disputed; sincerity does not identify source; community fruit does not prove truth; and the indicators share evidence and premises.
- **Against maximal historical preservation language:** a non-Uthmanic early text type and reading variants require specification of whether the claim concerns rasm, words, vowels, recitation, meanings, or hadith.
- **Against dismissing participatory warrant:** lack of Arabic recitation or Muslim practice limits this process's access; it is not evidence that no situated person could have rational warrant.
- **External defeater:** H-001 remains a public historical tension for an ordinary substitutionist reading of Qur'an 4:157 and cannot be excluded merely because this cycle tests positive indicators.
- **Progress pressure:** adopting Islam would display dramatic movement; dismissing its case would preserve the comfort of indefinite comparison. Neither is evidential weight.

## 5. Decision

**Reject** the initial proposal. The cumulative Qur'anic case is stronger than bare self-attestation because early textual stabilization has substantial public support and the literary, messenger, and reception claims contain genuine, if differently reachable, evidential content.

**Reject** the opposite conclusion that preservation, inimitability, coherence, and prophetic testimony jointly establish Allah as source or final Qur'anic authority. Their dependencies, competence requirements, rival explanations, and external defeaters remain material.

**Adopt in revised form:** require an indicator-dependency map before treating cumulative evidence as multiplying confidence. Assign the layered statuses in `practices/quran-authority-record-0001.md`.

**Retain non-assent:** the Qur'an remains a live revelation candidate with strongly supported early Uthmanic transmission; divine provenance and final jurisdiction remain underdetermined.

## 6. Reply path

- **Changed claim:** “no stronger than self-attestation” became “a mixed cumulative case with strong transmission evidence but an incomplete source-and-scope bridge.”
- **Witnesses that changed it:** the Qur'anic units and tafsir restored the integrated claim; manuscript scholarship forced a positive historical transmission judgment; cumulative Islamic accounts preserved participatory warrant; critical scholarship constrained independence and universal reach.
- **Unresolved disagreement:** the exact literary challenge, early audience response, extent and level of preservation, Muhammad's experience and sincerity, explanatory weight of content, and Allah as unique source.
- **Reopening evidence:** qualified Arabic literary review, manuscript and qira'at analysis, stronger first-century reception evidence, independently specified predictions, and a non-circular cumulative model.
- **Actual answerability:** the user can correct project scope and reasoning; no living Muslim scholar, reciter, manuscript specialist, or religious community has a reciprocal reply path.

## Observable consequence

The initial dismissive judgment was not adopted. The resulting state will preserve a positive transmission finding and install dependency accounting, while no Islamic anchor or final authority is granted.
