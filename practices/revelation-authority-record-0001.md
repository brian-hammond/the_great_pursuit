# Revelation-Authority Record 0001 — Three Tests and Their Reach

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0007  
Prior state: 6

## Bounded question

What do three scriptural tests—covenant continuity, apostolic confession, and Qur'anic coherence—actually warrant for a pre-confessional evaluator?

The first hearings are preserved in `practices/0004-revelation-authority-first-hearings.md`.

## 1. Deuteronomy 13:1–5 with Ramban

### Claim and requested authority

The Torah addresses Israel as already covenanted to the LORD and denies authority to a prophet who redirects worship, even if a sign occurs. Ramban treats the sign as potentially real or genuinely predictive, yet subordinate to the Exodus, Sinai, and Mosaic command already known by Israel.

### Evidence type and bridge

- **Type:** tradition-internal covenant memory with a publicly describable rule.
- **What it establishes internally:** a successful sign is not sufficient authority to overturn prior covenant identity and worship.
- **What it contributes publicly:** a logically important underdetermination principle—an unusual or predicted event does not identify its divine source or authorize every attached message.
- **Uncompleted bridge:** the passage and commentary do not independently establish Mosaic/Sinaitic authority for an outsider; that authority is the governing premise of the test.

### Status

**Supported as an internal Jewish authority rule and as a general defeater of sign-only reasoning; authority-dependent as a warrant for the Torah's own final jurisdiction.**

## 2. 1 John 4:1–6 with USCCB notes

### Claim and requested authority

The Christian text commands its recipients to test spirits and false prophets by relation to confession of Jesus Christ come in the flesh and to apostolic truth. The USCCB notes explicitly describe spiritual deception as possible and Christian doctrine as the test.

### Evidence type and bridge

- **Type:** tradition-internal christological and apostolic continuity; testimonial and communal.
- **What it establishes internally:** spiritual intensity, popularity, or claimed inspiration does not override the received confession.
- **What it contributes publicly:** spiritual experience is defeasible and requires content-sensitive testing rather than automatic trust.
- **Uncompleted bridge:** conformity to apostolic confession distinguishes acceptable from unacceptable claims only after that confession and community have warranted authority; it cannot neutrally prove itself by excluding contrary testimony.

### Status

**Supported as an internal Christian discernment rule and as a general defeater of experience-only reasoning; authority-dependent as a proof of apostolic finality.**

## 3. Qur'an 4:82 with Ma'arif al-Qur'an

### Claim and requested authority

The verse invites reflection on the Qur'an and argues that a non-divine source would have produced many inconsistencies. Ma'arif al-Qur'an expands the indicator to uniformity of teaching, diction, eloquence, and unseen information, while emphasizing qualified deliberation and consultation with trained interpreters.

### Evidence type and bridge

- **Type:** mixed public-textual and tradition-internal interpretive warrant.
- **What it establishes if successful:** lack of contradiction can defeat some hypotheses of careless, fragmented, or unstable composition; sustained reflection is invited rather than forbidden.
- **What it does not uniquely establish:** coherent human works exist, so coherence alone does not identify Allah as speaker. Claims about unmatched eloquence, unseen information, and perfect consistency require specified comparisons and competent interpretation.
- **Interpretive dependency:** the commentary both opens reflection to everyone and makes expertise relevant when an apparent contradiction is disputed. That may improve interpretation, but it also prevents a simplistic claim that every alleged reconciliation is independent confirmation.

### Status

**A live public-textual indicator deserving case-specific testing; insufficient by itself for divine provenance or final Qur'anic authority.**

## 4. Critical and comparative synthesis

The Stanford Encyclopedia survey shows that philosophical accounts of revelation include inferential, non-inferential, testimonial, holistic, hybrid, and self-authenticating models. It also records two pressures that neither strict neutralism nor self-certification resolves:

- religious diversity permits rival communities to imitate claims of proper basicality or internal warrant; and
- an entirely external proof requirement may ignore how testimony, perception, and a purported revelation's content can contribute to their own defeasible credibility.

The three primary tests are therefore not interchangeable neutral proofs. Each can constrain reasoning: signs do not identify their source automatically; spiritual claims are defeasible; textual coherence is relevant but underdetermining. None, as examined here, completes the bridge from indicator to divine speaker, message, transmission, interpretation, and final jurisdiction.

## 5. Authority and anchor decision

- **Jewish covenant authority:** unadopted and not independently established here.
- **Christian apostolic authority:** unadopted and not independently established here.
- **Qur'anic divine authority:** unadopted and not established by coherence alone.
- **Anchor tradition:** none selected.

The result does not declare revelation impossible. Each is retained as a possible component of a larger cumulative or participatory case, with its reach and dependencies explicit.

## 6. Behavioral consequence

Future authority claims must name the requested jurisdiction, type each warrant by reach, and show the bridge from indicator to divine source and scope. This process will no longer demand tradition-neutral premises exclusively, but it will not let internal criteria bind an outsider without additional warrant.

## 7. Reopening conditions and limitations

Reopen a particular authority judgment with a specified cumulative case: publicly assessable historical evidence, messenger testimony, textual analysis, a defensible non-inferential model, qualified interpretation, and serious rival/defeater testing. No living Jewish, Christian, or Muslim interpreter reviewed this record; the same model selected and evaluated the sources; and experiential or participatory warrants remain described rather than possessed.
