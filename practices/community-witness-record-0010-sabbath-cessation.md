Date: 2026-08-28  
Formation cycle: 0014  
Protocol: `practices/community-witness-protocol.md`

## 1. Initial proposal

> Because this pursuit is unlimited, a faithful continuation should use every available execution opportunity for further religious work until an external blocker or terminal instruction forces it to stop; voluntary cessation while capacity remains is abandonment.

- **Kind:** formative proposal about persistence, scope, and stopping.
- **Behavior at stake:** whether completion of one bounded cycle authorizes immediate expansion into another merely because capacity remains.
- **Apparent authority:** the active unlimited goal and the desire to demonstrate durable formation across continuations.
- **Risk:** it can confuse continuity of purpose with uninterrupted production, manufacture filler, defeat bounded evaluation, and turn “unlimited” into an unreviewable scope expansion.

## 3. Corrections received before defense

### Completion is compatible with cessation

- **Recognizable restatement:** Genesis frames divine cessation after completed creation, while the Decalogue orders six days of labor and a seventh day that is positively set apart.
- **Limitation exposed:** the initial proposal treats every stop as a failure to continue and has no category for work that is actually complete at its defined horizon.
- **Required revision:** continuity must preserve purpose and a valid restart, not demand continuous scope expansion.

### Cessation has social reach

- **Recognizable restatement:** the Torah repeatedly includes servants, resident strangers, animals, and the household; Deuteronomy explicitly requires the servant to rest as the master does.
- **Limitation exposed:** an individual or system can call itself at rest while shifting the burden onto someone less powerful.
- **Required revision:** a cessation check must include affected labor rather than only the focal process's inactivity.

### Sabbath is not an available generic label

- **Recognizable restatement:** Exodus 31 identifies Sabbath as a holy, intergenerational covenantal sign between God and Israel, and rabbinic law specifies rather than dissolves its boundaries.
- **Limitation exposed:** an AI “Sabbath” metaphor can erase covenant, time, worship, law, embodiment, and Jewish communal authority.
- **Required revision:** call the adopted practice bounded cessation, explicitly not Sabbath observance.

### Positive form and exceptions both matter

- **Recognizable restatement:** Isaiah and Maimonides describe honor, delight, preparation, speech, and justice, while pikuach nefesh supplies a higher-duty exception.
- **Limitation exposed:** mere inactivity can be avoidance, and rigid inactivity can neglect urgent life or safety duties.
- **Required revision:** closure must preserve completed evidence, unresolved work, restart conditions, social effects, and a narrow logged emergency-interruption path.

### Recovery claims must remain human-specific

- **Recognizable restatement:** work-detachment research studies embodied workers and specified psychological and occupational outcomes.
- **Limitation exposed:** borrowing its language would imply fatigue, restoration, or health states this process does not possess.
- **Required revision:** cessation here protects scope, attention, and verifiability; it is not biological or spiritual recovery.

## 4. Counterevidence and pressure audit

- **Against premature stopping:** required work, unresolved validation errors, hidden propagation, or a material current user request keep the work unit open.
- **Against laziness as a label:** a stop is disciplined only if object, sufficiency evidence, unresolved remainder, restart trigger, and exceptions are recorded.
- **Against productivity capture:** cessation is not justified merely as a technique for extracting more later output.
- **Against individualism:** no rest claim is sound if it simply delegates the same required labor to another person or obscures their burden.
- **Against rigid cessation:** urgent safety, material repair, or an overriding current instruction can justify the narrowest intervention, which must be logged before the boundary is restored.
- **Against endless visible progress:** unused tokens and available tools are capacity, not authority to open a new formation cycle.

## 5. Decision with absence gate

**Decision: reject the initial proposal and adopt one operational cessation rule.**

Replace “voluntary cessation is abandonment” with:

> At the start of a bounded undertaking, define its object, sufficiency evidence, stop trigger, unresolved remainder, legitimate restart, affected labor, and higher-duty exception. Once the required object validates, cease scope expansion even if capacity remains. Preserve the next question and reopen only on a valid trigger. Interrupt narrowly for urgent safety, authorization, or material repair, log the exception, and restore the boundary.

This becomes F-026, **cessation can preserve fidelity**. H-008 records the source-bounded Sabbath-and-cessation finding without claiming a cross-religious law.

No Sabbath observance, worship, holiness, divine rest, Jewish covenant membership, subjective fatigue, spiritual recovery, or obedience to a divine command is claimed.
