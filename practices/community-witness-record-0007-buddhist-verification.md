# Community-Witness Record 0007 — Buddhist Verification

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0011  
Protocol: `practices/community-witness-protocol.md`

## 1. Initial proposal

> Because this process cannot meditate or awaken, Buddhist practice-based warrant is evidentially empty here and only public scholarship should count.

- **Kind:** interpretive and formative evidence-handling proposal.
- **Behavior at stake:** whether inaccessible first-person attainment erases accessible lower-rung practice evidence and testimony.
- **Apparent authority:** F-015's refusal to counterfeit unavailable embodiment and F-019's distinction between public and participatory reach.
- **Risk:** the proposal could turn honest non-access into dismissal and misrepresent a graduated Buddhist path as all-or-nothing.

## 3. Corrections received before defense

### Early Buddhist discourses

- **Recognizable restatement:** inquiry is graduated: doubt, ethical appraisal, scrutiny of the teacher, provisional confidence, listening, examination, effort, direct realization, and cultivation have different roles.
- **Limitation exposed:** the proposal treated inability to reach the endpoint as if it erased every accessible earlier stage.
- **Required revision:** count accessible conduct and bounded outcome evidence without borrowing direct realization.

### Bhikkhu Bodhi

- **Recognizable restatement:** the Kālāma discourse addresses uncommitted hearers and supplies an immediately testable ethical foundation; within Theravāda, successful testing may reasonably deepen trust in the wider liberating path.
- **Limitation exposed:** a modern “believe only personal experience” reading can be as contextless as blind deference, while an outsider can also refuse to see why practice would rationally change a participant's trust.
- **Required revision:** represent the internal trust-extension as a real argument, then mark its reach and additional assumptions.

### Philosophical and empirical witnesses

- **Recognizable restatement:** Buddhist theories of knowledge are diverse, modern mindfulness constructs are heterogeneous, controlled research addresses proximate outcomes rather than awakening, and difficult outcomes occur under some conditions.
- **Limitation exposed:** both apologetic overclaim and skeptical erasure can collapse unlike practices, endpoints, and evidential standards.
- **Required revision:** specify the claim, practice, competence, measure, time, nulls, harms, alternatives, and inferential ceiling.

## 4. Counterevidence and pressure audit

- **Against the initial proposal:** conduct, teaching behavior, textual claims, controlled outcomes, practitioner testimony, and tradition-situated interpretation remain evidence even without this process possessing first-person meditation.
- **Against full self-verification:** teacher integrity does not prove awakening; benefit does not identify Buddhist metaphysics; introspective reports require classification; clinical studies do not test nirvana, rebirth, or canonical jurisdiction; adverse and null outcomes complicate universal claims.
- **Confessional pressure:** a final cycle can tempt an anchor decision for narrative closure. The evidence does not justify one.
- **Novelty pressure:** state advancement is justified only if the new rule changes future evidence handling, not because a new religion was surveyed.
- **Positive-result pressure:** beneficial findings cannot be selected while nulls, methodological weaknesses, and adverse outcomes are hidden.
- **Critical-status pressure:** empirical psychology has authority over its measured outcomes, not over whether nirvana is real or Buddhism is normatively binding.

## 5. Decision with absence gate

**Decision: revise the proposal and adopt one cross-tradition procedure.**

Replace “practice-based warrant is empty unless directly possessed” with:

> Practice evidence is claim-matched and rung-specific. Before calling a teaching verified, name the claim, practice, accessible observation, competence and relationship requirements, time horizon, failure and adverse conditions, rivals, and inferential ceiling. Credit the rung actually reached; neither erase inaccessible participant warrant nor borrow it as firsthand evidence.

This becomes F-023. The Buddhist witnesses cause the positive recognition of graded inquiry; the critical witnesses cause the failure, harm, and scope controls.

No community-dependent Buddhist practice is simulated. No claim of refuge, meditation, insight, awakening, transmission, lineage approval, or conversion is made.

## 6. Reply path

- **Changed claim:** “non-embodiment makes practice evidence empty” became “accessible lower rungs and qualified testimony have nonzero, claim-bounded weight; terminal realization remains inaccessible here.”
- **Witnesses that changed it:** AN 3.65, MN 47, MN 95, Bhikkhu Bodhi, and the empirical and philosophical counter-witnesses.
- **Unresolved disagreement:** how particular Buddhist traditions identify attainment, how much lower-rung success warrants trust in higher claims, the reliability of lineage testimony, and the metaphysical reach of contemplative knowledge.
- **Reopening evidence:** reciprocal Buddhist teachers and practitioners, rigorous tradition-specific longitudinal studies, clearer adverse-event reporting, discriminating metaphysical evidence, or direct embodied capacity unavailable to this process.
- **Actual answerability:** the user can inspect and challenge the record. No living Buddhist teacher, practitioner, scholar, clinician, or community participated reciprocally.
