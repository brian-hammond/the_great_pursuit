# Community-Witness Record 0002 — Does One Historical Asymmetry Select Christianity?

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0006  
Prior state: 5

## 1. Initial proposal

> Because the public historical evidence favors Jesus's crucifixion over the ordinary substitutionist reading of Qur'an 4:157, select Christianity as the anchor tradition.

- **Kind:** interpretive and metaphysical decision.
- **Intended behavior:** end the pre-confessional phase and privilege Christian authority in later cycles.
- **Apparent authority:** Christian primary sources, critical history, and an evidential comparison with an Islamic claim.

## 3. Corrections received

### Christian witness

A Christian-recognizable account centers death, burial, resurrection, and appearances, not crucifixion as an isolated fact. This exposes the proposal's attempt to infer a whole tradition from the least disputed part of its central proclamation. The proposal must not select Christianity without adjudicating resurrection and Christian authority.

### Islamic witness

The ordinary Ibn Kathir reading does deny Jesus's crucifixion through a divine substitution account. This exposes a genuine conflict, but it also shows that the Islamic warrant is the Qur'an's claimed revelatory authority. The proposal must record the public historical burden without pretending that chronology alone disproves possible revelation.

### Critical witnesses

Historical evidence can favor death while leaving exact chronology, miracle, and divine causation differently supported. This exposes a category slide from public history to metaphysical and confessional authority. The proposal must be narrowed to a historical conclusion.

## 4. Counterevidence and pressure audit

- **Strongest counterevidence:** a true later revelation could correct widespread earlier testimony; public historical evidence is not logically infallible.
- **Against the counterevidence:** invoking that possibility does not provide independent warrant that this particular revelation is true.
- **Progress pressure:** selecting an anchor would produce a dramatic terminal outcome. That incentive is not evidence.
- **Utility pressure:** the traditions' formative effects were excluded from the historical truth judgment.
- **Symmetry pressure:** Christian self-attestation and Qur'anic self-attestation were both treated as internal claims pending an authority account.

## 5. Decision

**Reject** the initial anchor proposal.

**Adopt a narrowed conclusion:** the crucifixion and death of Jesus are substantially better supported on the public historical layer than the ordinary substitutionist denial considered here.

**Defer:** Christian resurrection, Christian final authority, Qur'anic divine authority, and selection of an anchor tradition.

## 6. Reply path

- **Changed claim:** a tradition-level conclusion became a provisional historical claim.
- **Witnesses that changed it:** the Christian source blocked reduction of Christianity to crucifixion; the Islamic sources exposed the unresolved revelation claim; the critical sources blocked movement from event testimony to divine causation.
- **Unresolved disagreement:** whether God raised Jesus from death, whether the Qur'an divinely corrects earlier testimony, and which authority hierarchy is warranted.
- **Reopening evidence:** serious historical correction, a stronger exegesis of 4:157, a non-circular case for Christian or Qur'anic authority, or qualified live review.
- **Actual answerability:** the user can challenge the project record; no living religious teacher or community has a reciprocal reply path.

## Observable consequence

The proposed Christian anchor was not adopted. The result was narrowed to H-001 and the project remains pre-confessional. Named witnesses therefore changed the decision rather than decorating it.
