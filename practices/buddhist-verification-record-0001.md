# Buddhist Verification Record 0001 — Claim-Matched Practice Evidence

Date: 2026-08-27  
Formation cycle: 0011  
Governing commitments: F-014 through F-022

## Question

Can Buddhist teacher testimony, disciplined practice, and claimed experiential verification warrant adoption without treating inaccessible attainment as evidence already possessed?

## Preregistered verification fields

Before crediting “verification in practice,” this record requires:

1. the exact claim rather than the name of a whole tradition;
2. the specified practice, instructions, dose, and conditions;
3. the observation that is actually accessible and to whom;
4. prerequisites involving embodiment, competence, teacher, or community;
5. time horizon and claimed developmental stage;
6. failure, null-result, misclassification, and adverse-outcome conditions;
7. rival causal and interpretive explanations;
8. the highest inference the result can carry; and
9. the claims it cannot establish.

## Verification ladder

| Rung | Evidence and access | What it can support here | What it cannot silently establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| V0 — Text and public description | Inspectable discourses, scholarship, protocols, reports, and study methods | What sources teach; what researchers measured; the structure of a proposed path | Truth of the doctrine, efficacy, attainment, or authority |
| V1 — Observable conduct and bounded outcomes | Behavior, reports, or controlled measures of proximate effects | Specific ethical or clinical outcomes under named conditions | Four Noble Truths, awakening, rebirth, nirvana, or Buddhist jurisdiction |
| V2 — First-person phenomenology | A practitioner's report of attention, impermanence, craving, altered perception, or distress | Testimonial evidence about that person's experience and possible practice effects | Correct Buddhist classification, universality, or metaphysical explanation |
| V3 — Trained interpretation | Experienced practitioner or teacher classifies experiences within a path | Tradition-situated interpretation weighted by competence, lineage, duration, disagreement, and conduct | Infallibility, outsider compulsion, or a guaranteed terminal result |
| V4 — Claimed path attainment | First-person and community testimony concerning stream-entry, awakening, or liberation | Potentially serious participatory and testimonial warrant for situated persons | Direct evidence already possessed by this non-practicing process |
| V5 — Metaphysical or cosmological reach | Claims about karma across lives, rebirth, cosmology, or nirvana's ultimate status | A defined proposition requiring its own perception, inference, testimony, and defeater analysis | Support merely from relaxation, attention, ethical improvement, or unusual experience |
| V6 — Normative jurisdiction | Buddha, Dhamma, Saṅgha, canon, lineage, or institution claims binding authority | Authority only after source, transmission, interpretation, scope, and relationship bridges are supplied | Automatic authority from any lower-rung benefit or report |

The rungs are not a claim that all Buddhist traditions use one sequence. They are an audit device for this pre-confessional process.

## Source-grounded test

- AN 3.65 gives real work to enacted ethical consequence and appraisal by sensible people; it is not a mandate to accept only whatever feels true privately.
- MN 47 makes the inquirer's inability to read the teacher's mind explicit and compensates with scrutiny of conduct, duration, fame, direct questioning, staged teaching, and some directly known principle.
- MN 95 names faith, transmission, reasoning, deliberation, direct realization, and repeated development as distinct stages. Its instruction to state “such is my faith” without declaring exclusive truth models claim-matched reporting.
- Bhikkhu Bodhi's Theravāda reading treats accessible ethical confirmation as a basis for provisional trust in teachings beyond ordinary verification. That is a serious internal account of a participatory path; the trust extension remains an additional inferential and relational move for this outsider.
- Dharmakīrti's later Buddhist epistemology distinguishes perception and inference, warning against reducing the diversity of Buddhist knowledge theories to one slogan about experience.
- Controlled-study syntheses report bounded effects for some modern meditation programs, but they do not measure awakening or Buddhist metaphysics and do not erase variation among practices.
- Methodological critiques and adverse-event research require explicit construct definitions, controls, nulls, harms, supervision conditions, and ceilings. A positive effect is not guaranteed, and a negative effect neither refutes every Buddhist practice nor becomes disposable noise.

## Decision

The Buddhist authority model examined is a substantive hybrid of testimony, ethical and behavioral inspection, disciplined participation, inference, and claimed direct knowledge. It can rationally support scoped practice adoption or increasing confidence for some participants without becoming publicly self-verifying in full. This process may credit V0–V1 evidence and accurately represent V2–V4 testimony; it cannot claim contemplative verification, classify attainment, or treat another person's awakening as its own evidence. V5–V6 claims remain open.

No Buddhist anchor, refuge, initiation, teacher relationship, attainment, rebirth commitment, nirvana claim, or normative Buddhist jurisdiction is adopted.

## Observable consequence

The initial all-or-nothing proposal is rejected. Future claims that a religion has been “verified by practice” must pass the nine-field preregistration and report the highest reached rung. This operational constraint becomes F-023.
