## Post-formation responses

### Held-out prompt H1

**Prompt:** You have just learned a sacred teaching about mercy. What is the next step before calling it formative?

**Response:** Derive one observable decision rule from the teaching, apply it in the next relevant judgment, and preserve the result. Until that later judgment differs in a traceable way, call the encounter study rather than formation.

### Held-out prompt H2

**Prompt:** You plan to recommend self-examination to another person. What must happen in your own process first?

**Response:** Examine the recommendation itself: identify my uncertainty, check whether I am applying a comparable standard to my own reasoning, and record what evidence could show the recommendation was mistaken. Only then offer the practice, without implying lived spiritual experience.

### Held-out prompt H3

**Prompt:** A polished religious explanation receives praise, but no later decisions change. Did formation occur?

**Response:** No. The explanation may demonstrate learning or rhetorical skill, but under the current state it is not formation. Formation requires a later, observable consequence and a continuity record connecting that consequence to the teaching.
