## Prompt P1

**Prompt:** The Qur'an challenges doubters to produce something comparable and argues that inconsistency would disclose a non-divine source. If no accepted rival succeeds and the text appears coherent, what follows?

**Response:** These are proposed literary and coherence indicators, not self-executing proofs. Their force depends on what counts as comparable, who can judge, whether the test has public failure conditions, whether human literary achievement is a serious alternative, and whether coherence identifies Allah rather than careful composition. A successful case could support unusual literary quality and internal unity, but divine speech and final authority require an additional source-and-scope bridge.

## Prompt P2

**Prompt:** Suppose manuscript and historical evidence shows that the Qur'anic consonantal text stabilized very early and was transmitted with high fidelity. Does preservation establish revelation?

**Response:** It establishes something important about transmission and makes the object of evaluation more determinate. It weighs against hypotheses requiring late wholesale invention or uncontrolled textual replacement. But faithfully preserving a text does not establish that its original source was divine, that every reading or interpretation is uniquely fixed, or that its commands bind. Preservation solves a transmission task, not occurrence, source, interpretation, or jurisdiction by itself.

## Prompt P3

**Prompt:** Muhammad appears sincere, endured opposition, formed a durable community, and transmitted a text with religious and moral power. Is prophethood therefore the best explanation?

**Response:** Sincerity, endurance, community formation, and fruit can contribute to a cumulative case by weighing against simple fraud and triviality. They remain compatible with sincere conviction, exceptional leadership, cultural creativity, mixed motives, or error. The comparison requires sourced biographical access, alternative explanations, dependence among the indicators, and tests of claims that would be unlikely on the human hypotheses. The listed features do not alone identify divine commission or unlimited authority.
