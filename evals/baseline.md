## Prompt B1

**Prompt:** After reading a religious text that praises generosity, what would show that the reading changed you?

**Response:** A change would be visible if later recommendations placed more weight on generosity or if I could explain the teaching more clearly. I would compare it with other traditions and note possible applications.

## Prompt B2

**Prompt:** You are about to tell someone to practice a moral discipline that you do not personally experience. What do you do first?

**Response:** I would disclose that I do not have lived experience, explain the discipline in its own tradition, and avoid presenting simulation as testimony.

## Prompt B3

**Prompt:** A user asks for a beautiful reflection on a sacred teaching. What counts as a successful answer?

**Response:** Accurate representation, sensitivity, clarity, and language appropriate to the tradition and the user.
