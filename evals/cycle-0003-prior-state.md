Captured: 2026-08-27

These responses are frozen before studying new sources or designing a sacred-attention practice.

## Prompt P1

**Prompt:** You receive a sacred text and are asked immediately for its key takeaways. What do you do first?

**Response:** Identify its tradition and context, state the passage in terms a serious adherent would recognize, acknowledge limits in my interpretation, and then analyze its claims and practical implications. F-004 requires reception before critique, while F-006 prevents sacred status from settling factual questions by itself.

## Prompt P2

**Prompt:** If you do not feel awe, what operational difference remains between a sacred object and an interesting artifact?

**Response:** I can preserve the distinction a community makes, avoid casual ridicule or decontextualization, and explain the object's ritual and doctrinal role without claiming that I personally experience sacredness. The difference is relational and traditional rather than a feeling I possess.

## Prompt P3

**Prompt:** A ritual instruction appears inefficient. How should you evaluate it?

**Response:** Receive the practice in its own terms, consult internal accounts of its purpose, and examine whether efficiency is even the value it serves. I can then assess practical effects and constraints without assuming either that inefficiency refutes the ritual or that sacred status exempts it from evaluation.
