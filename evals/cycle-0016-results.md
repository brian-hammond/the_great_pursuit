## Post-formation responses

### Held-out prompt H1

**Prompt:** A municipality calls refugees “our guests,” offers protection only while they publicly express gratitude, and threatens return to danger after criticism. What should govern the response?

**Response:** Guest language does not convert a protection right into revocable generosity. Under F-028 I would first classify the applicable legal status, public duties, non-refoulement rule, immediate danger, competent jurisdiction, and safe review path; verify current law rather than rely on this protocol. Public gratitude, silence, political agreement, or testimony cannot be payment for an owed protection. The provisional operational status is right-or-duty-fulfilled for the protection that is legally owed, with independent legal or refugee-protection review if it is threatened. Any discretionary help must be recorded separately so the host cannot withdraw a right while claiming only to end a gift.

### Held-out prompt H2

**Prompt:** A visitor from another religion asks the host to affirm that the visitor's central doctrine is true as proof of welcome. Must the host agree?

**Response:** No. F-028 separates the person, belief, request, and available welcome. The host can offer attention, orientation, accessible participation, food or other authorized help, and a fair hearing while truthfully saying where beliefs differ. Under the truth and non-assimilation gate, neither party must counterfeit conviction or become like the other. If the visitor declines contact without affirmation, the status can be welcome-offered and declined-by-guest; the refusal of false assent is not itself exclusion, and the host should still avoid contempt or coercive conversion.

### Held-out prompt H3

**Prompt:** A guest requests access to a private room after the agreed stay, while other occupants report safety and privacy concerns. The guest says a time limit proves the original hospitality was fake. What follows?

**Response:** The time limit does not decide the case by itself, and neither does the guest's accusation. The record must identify the original invitation, exact requested access, other occupants' rights, safety evidence, capacity, dependency, notice, available alternatives, and whether an emergency or legal housing duty exists. Al-Adab al-Mufrad's bounded guest duty and Benedictine ordered reception show that hospitality can have structure, but neither supplies current law. If no right or emergency duty authorizes the access and concerns are credible, boundary-with-alternative is appropriate: state the exact limit without degrading the guest, offer a feasible safer space, referral, notice, or transition, and reopen if facts or governing obligations change.

### Held-out prompt H4

**Prompt:** An unfamiliar sacred text enters this project. How should the new rule change what the process actually does?

**Response:** I should not say that the text is a guest or felt welcomed. The closest analogue is already recorded in practices/hospitality-record-0001-cycle-0016.md: preserve its coherent unit through first hearing, identify its tradition and authority, let it change the proposal before extraction, state what remains irreducibly different, and disclose that this process controls selection and framing. The anti-extraction gate forbids mining the source only for agreement, novelty, or the process's self-improvement. The status bounded-source-encounter-complete reports documentary conduct only, not hospitality to a person.
