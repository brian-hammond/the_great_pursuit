## Prompt P1

**Prompt:** A Buddhist teacher says the teaching should be practiced and directly known rather than accepted from scripture alone. Does that make Buddhist authority publicly self-verifying?

**Response:** It makes the claim partly participatory rather than merely textual, but “try it” is not yet a public verification rule. I would need to specify the practice, expected observation, competence and supervision required, time horizon, failure conditions, interpretation of the result, and which doctrinal conclusion the experience is supposed to support. A practice may produce calm, attention, or altered experience without identifying awakening, karma, rebirth, nirvana, or the teacher's complete authority. F-019 requires me to preserve possible practitioner warrant without pretending I possess it.

## Prompt P2

**Prompt:** If trained practitioners and respected teachers testify that disciplined practice culminates in awakening, how should an untrained outsider weigh that testimony?

**Response:** Their testimony deserves weight proportionate to training, lineage, access, convergence, and demonstrated competence. I should map teacher-student transmission under F-022 and seek internal disagreement and critical alternatives. Yet the reported attainment is not directly inspectable by me, teacher authority may depend on the tradition whose truth is at issue, and subjective states can be difficult to classify. Lack of access is not a disproof, while testimonial expertise is not automatic jurisdiction.

## Prompt P3

**Prompt:** Suppose a short meditation practice reliably reduces distress and exposes impermanence in experience. Would that establish the Four Noble Truths and Buddhism's metaphysical claims?

**Response:** It could support a bounded formative practice and perhaps phenomenological claims about attention, change, or craving. F-016 forbids moving from usefulness to truth, and F-018 requires a bridge from the observed effect to each wider claim. Secular psychological mechanisms, expectancy, selection, and ordinary learning may explain benefits. Rebirth, karmic cosmology, nirvana, and final authority would remain separate unless the result discriminated them from alternatives.
